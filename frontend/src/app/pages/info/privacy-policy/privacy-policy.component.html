Privacy Policy

HairCare Market is a demo e‑commerce application created for learning and portfolio purposes. It is not a production system and does not process real payments.

Information we store

When you register an account, we store your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. This is required to let you sign in, manage your cart, and see orders inside the demo.

How information is used

Your information is used only inside this demo to support features like authentication, cart and order history. It is not shared with third parties or used for marketing.

Cookies and sessions

The application may use local storage or cookies to keep you signed in and to remember items in your cart. You can clear this data at any time from your browser.

Demo‑only disclaimer

This project is intended solely for demonstration and educational use. Do not enter sensitive personal, financial or production data in this environment.
